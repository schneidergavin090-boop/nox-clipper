Nox Clipper – Privacy Policy

Nox Clipper is used only by its owner. It does not collect, sell or share personal data.

The TikTok access token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is used solely to upload the owner's own videos as drafts to the owner's own account. No data is transmitted to third parties.

The authorization can be revoked at any time in the TikTok app settings.
